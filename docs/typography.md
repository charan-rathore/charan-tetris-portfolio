# Typography and legibility

IBM Plex Sans is the main reading and heading face. Its open counters and differentiated letterforms keep small text clear, while the variable weights give headings emphasis without making every word an arcade graphic. It is self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License. Geist Mono stays with clocks and measurements. Press Start 2P is limited to short game labels.

This is an editorial choice, not an AI-authorship detector. No typeface or punctuation proves who wrote a sentence. The review checks readable line lengths, hierarchy, contrast, glyph clipping and mobile wrapping on the actual interface.
